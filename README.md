
# India EV Market Intelligence & Growth Opportunity Analysis

## AtliQ Motors — India Market Entry Study

## Business Problem
Brief description of AtliQ Motors and the India expansion decision.

## Project Objective
- Identify attractive states
- Measure EV growth and penetration
- Evaluate competitors
- Assess market maturity
- Recommend market-entry priorities

## Dataset
Three monthly datasets covering FY2022–FY2024:

| Dataset                                           | Purpose |
|---|---|
| electric_vehicle_sales_by_state.csv               | State-level EV and total vehicle sales |
| electric_vehicle_sales_by_makers.csv              | Manufacturer-level EV sales |
| dim_date.csv                                      | Fiscal year and quarter mapping |

Raw files are available in the `data/` folder.

## Tools
SQL | Python | Excel | Power Query | GitHub

## Analytical Approach

### 1. State-Level EV Opportunity Score
Market Size + EV Growth + Penetration Gap + Penetration Momentum

### 2. Market Opportunity Ranking & Classification
States ranked and classified by opportunity.

### 3. Market Maturity Matrix
States classified into:
- High-Potential
- Emerging
- Mature
- Low-Priority

### 4. Manufacturer Competitive Intelligence
Market share, YoY growth and competitive momentum.

### 5. EV Growth & Market Structure
Market Size Effect vs EV Adoption Effect.

### 6. Market Concentration
CR5 and HHI.

### 7. Statistical Analysis
Descriptive statistics, correlations and outlier analysis.

## Key Findings

### EV Market Growth
EV penetration increased from 1.65% in FY2022 to 4.81% in FY2024.

### State Opportunity
Uttar Pradesh emerged as the strongest opportunity based on the composite opportunity framework.

### Competitive Landscape
OLA ELECTRIC led FY2024 with 31.63% market share.

### Market Concentration
CR5 = 77.99%; HHI = 1,660.87 → Moderately Concentrated Market.

### Vehicle Category
2W dominates EV volumes, while 4W recorded faster CAGR during FY2022–FY2024.

### Seasonality
March recorded the highest EV demand in the available data.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Prioritize large, underpenetrated markets.
2. Use differentiated positioning in mature EV markets.
3. Treat high-growth small states as pilot markets.
4. Evaluate 2W and 4W through separate strategies.
5. Plan inventory and marketing around seasonal demand.
6. Compete through differentiation rather than volume alone.

## Final Market Entry Framework

Tier 1 → Large + underpenetrated + growing  
Tier 2 → Established EV markets  
Tier 3 → High-growth smaller markets  
Tier 4 → Low-priority markets

Conclusion:

India's EV market is expanding rapidly. The analysis suggests that AtliQ Motors should not treat India as one homogeneous market. State-level market size, EV adoption, growth, maturity and competitive intensity should guide the market-entry strategy.

Enter where the market is large enough to matter, EV adoption is accelerating, and AtliQ Motors can build a defensible competitive advantage.
